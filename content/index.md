>**temenos** ⠀·⠀ τέμενος⠀  _noun ⠀·⠀ Ancient Greek_  
> A sacred precinct; a space set apart from ordinary use and dedicated to a deity. From τέμνω (_temnō_), “to cut.” 

In Jungian thought, the _temenos_ is a symbolic enclosure that protects an inner psychic process. I borrow this image for the garden: a space set apart for reflection and imaginative work, where the materials of an interior life can be gathered, considered, and gradually brought into relation. Here, I collect an assortment of notes, fragments, and ongoing inquiries, synced from Obsidian. Some entries remain close to their sources; others become occasions for writing, making, or reconsidering what I thought I understood. I leave them here to accumulate associations and to be encountered again as my understanding changes.
